# PEACE Protocol Trademark and Compatibility Policy

Status: publication proposal, 2026-08-19

## 1. Principle

The protocol is open. The name, logos and certification marks are separate.

Apache License 2.0 grants rights to use, modify and redistribute the covered work. It does not grant trademark rights beyond reasonable descriptive reference.

## 2. Descriptive use

Accurate descriptive use of `PEACE Protocol` is permitted to identify:

- the protocol implemented;
- the specification version tested;
- interoperability/conformance work;
- independent analysis, discussion or comparison;
- forks/derivatives that clearly identify themselves as unofficial.

Descriptive use must not imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement or control by VALO.

## 3. Compatibility claims

### `PEACE-compatible`

May be used for an implementation that:

1. identifies the exact protocol version/profile;
2. passes the mandatory public conformance vectors for the claimed profile;
3. states the test date/method;
4. identifies material extensions/deviations;
5. does not imply official certification.

Recommended form:

> Self-tested PEACE-compatible with Protocol vX.Y, profile Z. Not officially certified or endorsed.

### `PEACE-conformant`

May be used only where every mandatory requirement for the claimed version/profile is satisfied and the implementation publishes a conformance statement sufficient for independent reproduction.

This remains a self-declaration unless an authorized certification programme performed the assessment.

### `PEACE Certified`

Reserved for a certification programme formally operated or authorized by the PEACE trademark/certification authority.

No self-certification may use `Certified`, an official badge, or confusingly similar endorsement language.

## 4. Forks and derivatives

Forks and derivative works are permitted under the applicable licence but MUST:

- preserve required licence/NOTICE material;
- identify material changes;
- avoid presenting the fork as the official PEACE protocol;
- use a distinct project/product name if the protocol semantics diverge materially;
- avoid official PEACE logos/badges without permission.

## 5. No vendor capture through branding

Trademark control MUST NOT be used to prevent truthful statements that an independent implementation implements, tests against or interoperates with the public PEACE specification.

Trademark control protects source/certification meaning; it is not a mechanism to create a licence fee or mandatory intermediary.

## 6. Certification mark separation

If a `PEACE Certified` programme is introduced, its requirements SHALL be documented separately from the open protocol licence.

Certification may require stronger operational, assurance, security, recovery, audit or support controls than bare protocol conformance.

Failure or refusal to buy certification does not remove the right to implement the Apache-licensed protocol.

## 7. Ownership transition

Before the dedicated repository becomes canonical, final ownership/registration wording for the `PEACE` name and any certification marks MUST be confirmed and updated here.

If trademark ownership later transfers to a foundation or standards body, the transfer and effective date must be explicit.
