# PEACE World Contract v0

**Personal Execution, Authority & Compute Environment**  
**Your Sovereign State.**

This document defines the world PEACE must make possible. It deliberately does **not** prescribe an implementation, programming language, vendor, operating system, cryptographic stack, model, device, storage engine, or runtime architecture.

Imagine a world where a person or organisation continuously changes phones, AI models, agents, clouds, devices, runtimes, services and compute providers. Any of them may fail, disappear, be replaced, become compromised, or become unavailable at any time.

The sovereign domain must nevertheless remain the same logical actor and retain control of identity, authority, authoritative state, rights, relationships, delegations, governance, history and recovery path.

External intelligence and compute may receive only what is needed for a specific purpose. They may reason, calculate, simulate, recommend and propose. Possessing data, producing a plan, performing computation, authenticating successfully, routing a task, hosting state or being selected as a provider must never by itself grant authority over the sovereign domain.

Some proposals may lead to real-world consequences. Between proposal and consequence, state, authority, delegation, scope, purpose, permissions, revocation status and external conditions may change.

Evidence of what was observed, proposed, authorized, attempted and produced must survive replacement or loss of any particular worker, device, model, runtime or provider. Evidence may be valid and tamper-evident without automatically becoming authoritative state or truth.

Every implementation artifact is replaceable, including programming language, runtime, device, provider, model, credential and cryptographic key. Replacing such artifacts must not by itself replace the actor or transfer the source of authority to the replacement artifact.

Loss of a replaceable component may reduce capability. It must not destroy constitutional control of the operative domain.

## Derivation question

Given only these conditions:

> **What must necessarily be true for this world to remain sovereign, safe and reconstructible?**
>
> Derive the smallest set of invariants, boundaries and state transitions that makes the world possible. Prefer the path of least resistance: the simplest architecture that satisfies all conditions without relying on a particular programming language, operating system, vendor, model, device, credential scheme or existing implementation.

A PEACE design is stronger when independent implementations can derive equivalent semantics from this world contract without reading another implementation's source code.
